## Rebuilding the textures from the originals

`tools/prep-assets.py` expects the originals under `assets/photos/<set>/` and the cutouts under `assets/*-cutouts/` (made by `assets/make-cutouts.py` with the BiRefNet-general ONNX model); it writes `assets/tex/` and `data/cutouts.js`, running every texture through `tools/debrand.py` first. Neither the originals nor the cutouts are in the repository; download the files above to rebuild.
